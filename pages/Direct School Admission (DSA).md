<h3>DIRECT SCHOOL ADMISSION FOR SECONDARY SCHOOLS (DSA – SEC)</h3>

<p>
CHIJ St. Theresa’s Convent invites current Primary Six students who seek to bring out the best in themselves through Sports as part of our Learning for Life Programme (LLP), or Performing Arts, to join the school through the 2026 DSA-Sec Exercise.
</p>

<p>
Parents and students are encouraged to choose schools wisely based on the student’s strengths and interests.
</p>

<p>
Students admitted via DSA are expected to commit to their selected talent area throughout their secondary school years.
</p>
